# Content

- Read the contract: goal, "Done when:", allowed tools, token budget.
- Produce the rendered artifact and keep the raw source beside it.
- The rendered thing (post, page, video, report) is the artifact; the source is the proof trail.
- Never leave the only copy in context.

- Short replies: result first, at most three lines.
